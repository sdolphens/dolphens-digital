Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2026

Dolphens Digital is a small content marketing agency based in Omaha, Nebraska. This policy explains, in plain English, what information we collect through this website and what we do with it.

What we collect

If you book a call through our scheduling calendar or email us, we receive the contact information you choose to share — typically your name, email address, phone number, business name, and anything you tell us about your business. That's it. We don't collect anything you don't hand us.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, prepare for and follow up on calls, and provide our services if you become a client. We may email you about your inquiry or your account. We don't add you to marketing lists you didn't ask for.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. Ever.

Third-party services

Call scheduling on this site is handled by Calendly, which processes the information you enter when booking. Calendly's own privacy policy governs how it handles that data. Email sent to us is processed by our email provider, like any business email.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep contact information only as long as it's useful for working with you. If you'd like us to delete what we have about you, ask and we will.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, or you want to know what information we have about you, email hello@dolphensdigital.com and you'll get an answer from a real person.
