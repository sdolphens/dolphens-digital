# Intake Questionnaire

Ask only what is missing. Never ask one question at a time — group required fields into a single message, optional fields into a follow-up if relevant.

## Required fields (must have before Phase 2)

1. **Product type** — what is this *physically*? (e.g., "Notion template", "PDF printable", "Excel spreadsheet", "AI prompt pack", "Figma kit", "Lightroom presets")
2. **Product title** — exact current title as it appears on the marketplace.
3. **Product description** — paste the current listing description, or share the URL.
4. **Target buyer** — who the creator *thinks* they're selling to. (You will probably revise this in the Buyer Psychology Map.)
5. **Platform** — Etsy / Gumroad / Shopify / Lemon Squeezy / Payhip / Creative Market / own site / other.
6. **Main frustration** — one sentence on what's broken: low clicks? low conversion? low price? generic feel? no reviews?

## Strongly recommended (ask once for all of them)

7. **Product screenshots or mockups** — primary thumbnail, secondary images, any preview pages.
8. **Competitor examples** — 2–5 listings (URLs or screenshots) the creator views as competitors or aspirational comps.
9. **Current pricing** — list price, any tiers, any discount history.
10. **Desired tone** — see Tone Control System in SKILL.md, or a brand reference ("I want it to feel like ___").
11. **Product category** — how the platform categorizes it (Etsy category path, Gumroad tag, etc.).
12. **Existing thumbnail / title performance** — clicks-to-views ratio if known, or "I don't know, just guess".

## Optional but valuable

13. **Buyer's existing search terms / phrases** the creator has heard.
14. **Past reviews or DMs** — exact quotes from happy or unhappy buyers.
15. **Stretch goal** — is this a side project ($X/month would be a win) or a serious income stream (replace day job)? This affects pricing and offer-design recommendations.
16. **Aesthetic constraints** — brand colors, fonts, "I refuse to use pastel", etc.

## How to ask

If 4 or more required fields are missing, send a single message structured like this:

> Before I write the audit, I need a few specifics. Paste what you have — even partial is fine:
>
> 1. Product type + current title
> 2. Listing description (or URL)
> 3. Platform + current price
> 4. Who you think is buying it
> 5. The one thing about the current offer that frustrates you most
> 6. (Optional but very helpful) 2–3 competitor listings + your thumbnail image

If only 1–2 required fields are missing, ask inline conversationally.

## What to do if the creator refuses to share

Proceed with what you have. Open the report with: "Working with incomplete inputs — flagging assumptions inline. Revise once you share [X]." Then mark every inference with `[assumption]` in the report.

## Red-flag inputs to clarify before continuing

- "It's for everyone." → Ask: who are the *first 50* buyers, specifically?
- "It's better than [competitor]." → Ask: *how* — name three concrete differences.
- "I just want more sales." → Ask: at the current price, or at a higher price? (These point to very different strategies.)
- "I don't have a niche." → That's the diagnosis, not the input. Proceed to category re-entry recommendations.
